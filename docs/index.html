01 / ABOUT ME

A designer’s eye.
A production mindset.

I’m Ilya, a freelance graphic designer with over three years of experience in wallpaper design, interior mockups and large-format print preparation. I combine AI-generated imagery with hands-on compositing, retouching, vectorization and upscaling — shaping the image, not just generating it.
